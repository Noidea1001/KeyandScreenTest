# 📝 Changelog — All-in-One Hardware Diagnostic Suite

## [v1.0.0] - 2026-11-11
### Added
- **Keyboard Tester**: Full 104-key, TKL 87-key, 75% 84-key, and 60% 61-key layouts with Key Chatter (<28ms) and NKRO rollover detection.
- **Mouse Tester**: 5-button click counter, scroll wheel tester, double-click fault detector, live Polling Rate (Hz) meter, and drag-and-drop sensor canvas.
- **Screen Diagnostics**: Fullscreen 8-color dead/stuck pixel test, backlight bleed & grey uniformity test, gradient banding test, and live FPS/Hz motion blur test.
- **Audio, Mic & Webcam Suite**: Left/Right/Stereo/Bass sweep generator, live microphone frequency visualizer, and webcam resolution checker.
- **Gamepad Tester**: Real-time controller button & analog axis drift monitor.
- **Auto-PASS & 10-Minute Auto-Reset Engine**: Automatic `PASS ✅` banner with audio fanfare, batch counter, CSV export, and configurable auto-reset countdown (default 10 minutes).
